##### **Test Sentences**



These sentences are used as fixed test cases for evaluating the natural-language-to-scene-generation pipeline.



###### Basic Objects



1\. A candle is on a table.



2\. A chair is near a table.



3\. A lamp is behind a chair.



###### Spatial Relationships



4\. A small candle is **on** a large wooden table.



5\. A chair is **near** a locked chest.



6\. A key is **on** the table **near** a locked chest.



###### Object States



7\. A **locked** chest is near a table.



8\. A **lit** candle is on a table.



###### Interaction Rules



9\. Lighting the candle **unlocks** the chest.



10\. If the candle is lit, the locked chest becomes **unlocked**.



###### Combined Scene



11\. A small candle is on a large table near a locked chest. Lighting the candle unlocks the chest.



12\. A key is on a table behind a chair. The key can be used to unlock a nearby chest.
