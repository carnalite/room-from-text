**Research Goal**



This project investigates whether natural-language descriptions of simple game environments can be transformed into a structured intermediate representation and subsequently interpreted by a game engine to generate spatial scenes and basic interactive rules.



The prototype focuses on extracting objects, spatial relationships, object states, and simple interaction rules from natural-language descriptions. A local language model is used for semantic interpretation, while Unity serves as the environment for scene generation and interaction.



The initial prototype intentionally uses primitive geometric representations rather than detailed assets, allowing the experiment to focus on the relationship between language, structured representation, spatial reasoning, and game-engine execution.
